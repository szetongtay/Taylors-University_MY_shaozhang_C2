Project Title: Food Hunter
Problem:
People experience daily "decision fatigue" when trying to figure out what to eat. Choosing a meal requires balancing multiple factors—budget, time of day, group size, and dietary restrictions—which can be a frustrating and time-consuming process.
Solution:
Food Hunter is an intelligent meal recommendation web application. Users simply input their current constraints: Meal Time (Lunch, Dinner, etc.), number of people, budget range (in RM), and dietary preferences (Halal, Vegetarian, etc.). The system then instantly "hunts" and generates the perfect meal recommendation, complete with price and location details.
Value:
Saves Time: Eliminates the endless back-and-forth of "what should we eat?" with a fun, one-click solution.
Personalized: Ensures the recommendation strictly fits the user's budget, group size, and dietary needs, preventing bad choices.
Local & Secure: As stated on our welcome page, the process is designed to run completely locally and securely, prioritizing user privacy.
Role of TideCloak:
TideCloak serves as our decentralized identity and data protection layer. We utilized TideCloak to:
Ensure that sensitive user data (like dietary restrictions and budget preferences) is handled securely.
Protect user privacy without relying on a centralized database that could be vulnerable to breaches.
Provide a secure, trustless environment for the food-picking process.
